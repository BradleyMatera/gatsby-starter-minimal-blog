# Matera Digital web brand assets

The Matera Digital artwork supplied for the Google Business Profile is the visual source of truth for this site.

## Canonical assets

- `/static/brand/matera-digital-logo.png` — transparent full logo for the footer and larger brand placements
- `/static/brand/matera-digital-mark.png` — transparent MD monogram for navigation
- `/static/brand/matera-digital-icon-1024.png` — square icon master on white for favicons and installed-app icons
- `/static/brand/matera-digital-social-1200x630.jpg` — default Open Graph and social-link preview image
- `/static/brand/bradley-matera-portrait.webp` — optimized owner portrait for compact trust placements

## Generated browser assets

Browser and installed-app icons are derived from the square icon master. Keep the monogram centered with generous white padding so it remains legible after browser and operating-system masking.

- `/static/favicon.ico`
- `/static/favicon-16x16.png`
- `/static/favicon-32x32.png`
- `/static/apple-touch-icon.png`
- `/static/android-chrome-192x192.png`
- `/static/android-chrome-512x512.png`
- `/static/maskable-icon-512x512.png`
- `/static/mstile-150x150.png`

## Core palette

- Matera navy: `#052a5d`
- White logo surface: `#ffffff`

Keep logo artwork on a white surface in themed UI so the navy portion remains readable in both light and dark modes. Do not recolor, stretch, rotate, or typeset a replacement for the supplied mark.
