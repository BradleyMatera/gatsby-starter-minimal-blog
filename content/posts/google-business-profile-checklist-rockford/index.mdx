If you run a business in Rockford and you show up in the map pack, you get calls. If you don't, you don't. Google Business Profile is the single highest-leverage local SEO task for any business with a physical location or service area. This checklist walks through every field that matters, in the order you should complete it.

## The short answer

Claim or create your Google Business Profile, verify it by postcard or video, pick the most specific primary category you qualify for, fill out every field with accurate information, upload real photos, post updates at least monthly, collect and respond to reviews, and answer questions in the Q&A section. Most Rockford businesses that rank in the local pack have done all of this. Most that don't rank have skipped at least two.

## Step 1: Claim or create your listing

Go to [business.google.com](https://business.google.com) and search for your business name. If it already exists, request ownership. If it doesn't, create it.

You will need:
- A Google account (use a shared business email, not a personal one)
- Your business name exactly as it appears on your signage and website
- Your real physical address (even if you hide it later as a service-area business)
- A phone number that reaches a human during business hours

### Verification

Google will ask you to verify. The most common methods:

| Method | How it works | Timeline |
|--------|-------------|----------|
| Postcard | Google mails a card with a 5-digit code to your address | 5-14 days |
| Video | You record a short video matching your location to Maps | 1-5 days |
| Phone | Google calls with a code (rare, only for some businesses) | Immediate |
| Email | Code sent to your business email domain (some businesses) | Immediate |

For Rockford businesses with a storefront, the postcard method is most common. If you are a service-area business working out of your home, Google may require video verification. Have your license, utility bill, or equipment with your business name visible.

## Step 2: Choose your primary category

This is the most important field on your entire profile. Google uses it to decide which searches you are eligible for.

Pick the most specific category that is accurate. "Plumber" is better than "Contractor." "HVAC contractor" is better than "Home improvement."

You can add up to 9 additional categories, but the primary one carries the most weight. Do not add categories you don't actually offer. Google can suspend your listing for miscategorization.

### Rockford-specific tip

Search Google Maps for your category in Rockford before you pick. Type "plumber Rockford IL" and see who ranks. Look at their categories to confirm yours matches what Google associates with the map pack for your service.

## Step 3: Fill out every field

Google does not penalize you for leaving fields blank, but it does reward you for filling them out. Complete profiles rank better and convert better.

### Business information

- **Name:** Your legal or commonly used name. Do not add keywords like "Rockford Plumber - 24/7 Emergency." Google will suspend you for keyword stuffing the name.
- **Address:** Your real address. If you are a service-area business, you will hide this in the next step.
- **Service area:** List the cities and ZIP codes you actually serve. For Rockford, that might include Loves Park, Machesney Park, Belvidere, Cherry Valley, and Rockford proper. Do not list cities you don't serve.
- **Hours:** Set your real hours. If you offer 24/7 emergency service, add those as separate hours.
- **Phone:** Use your primary business number. This must match your website and every directory listing. See my guide on [NAP consistency](/local-citations-nap-consistency/) for why this matters.

### Description

You get 750 characters. Use them. Mention what you do, who you serve, and what makes you different. Do not stuff keywords. Write it like you are talking to a customer.

Bad: "Rockford plumber providing plumbing services in Rockford IL for all your plumbing needs."

Good: "Family-owned plumbing company serving Rockford and the surrounding area since 2012. We handle drain cleaning, water heater installation, repipes, and emergency repairs. Licensed in Illinois. Free estimates on most jobs."

## Step 4: Add photos

Profiles with more photos get more views and more direction requests. Upload:

- **Cover photo:** A clean exterior or logo shot
- **Exterior photos:** So customers can find you
- **Interior photos:** Show your workspace or showroom
- **Team photos:** People buy from people
- **Product or work photos:** Before and after shots if you do service work
- **Logo:** PNG with transparent background

Aim for at least 20 photos. Update them quarterly. Google rewards fresh content.

## Step 5: Enable messaging

GBP has a built-in chat feature. Turn it on. Customers, especially on mobile, will message you instead of calling. Respond within 24 hours or Google will show a "slow to respond" label.

If you can't monitor it, set up auto-replies with your phone number and email. But actually responding is better.

## Step 6: Collect and respond to reviews

Reviews are the second biggest ranking factor after your profile completeness and category. You need both quantity and recency.

### How to get reviews
- Ask every satisfied customer in person
- Send a follow-up text with your review link
- Add a review link to your email signature and invoices
- Do not offer incentives. Google will remove reviews that were paid for.

### How to respond
- Respond to every review, positive and negative
- For positive reviews: thank them and mention something specific
- For negative reviews: acknowledge the issue, offer to fix it, take it offline
- Never argue with a reviewer publicly

A Rockford HVAC company with 80 reviews and a 4.7 average will outrank one with 12 reviews and a 5.0 average. Volume matters.

## Step 7: Post updates

GBP posts are short updates that show up on your profile. They expire after 6 months but they signal to Google that your profile is active.

Post types:
- **What's new:** Announcements, seasonal reminders
- **Offers:** Discounts, coupons
- **Events:** Open houses, workshops
- **Products:** Highlight specific services

Post at least once a month. Once a week is better. Include a photo and a call to action.

## Step 8: Answer Q&A

Customers can ask questions on your profile. If you don't answer them, anyone can. That includes competitors and random people.

Pre-seed your Q&A with the questions you get asked most:
- "Do you offer free estimates?"
- "What areas do you serve?"
- "Are you licensed and insured?"
- "Do you offer emergency service?"

Write the question and answer it yourself. This controls the narrative and gives searchers the information they need without calling.

## Common mistakes I see in Rockford

1. **Using a home address as a storefront** when you don't see customers there. Use service-area settings instead.
2. **Keyword stuffing the business name.** Google suspends for this.
3. **No photos.** A profile with zero photos looks abandoned.
4. **Ignoring reviews.** Unanswered negative reviews tell Google and customers you don't care.
5. **No posts in 6 months.** An inactive profile gets deprioritized.
