## The Northwest Illinois web development market

Northwest Illinois is not Chicago. The businesses here are not competing for national search terms. They are competing for the person in Durand, or Davis, or Freeport, or Rockford who searches "plumber near me" or "landscaping Durand IL" and picks from the first few results that look trustworthy.

That changes what a website needs to do. A business in this region does not need a complex web application. It needs a site that loads fast, shows up in local search, lists services clearly, and makes it obvious how to call or visit. Most of the sites I see in this area fail at one or more of those things — they are slow, they have no local schema markup, the phone number is buried, or the site was built from a template that looks like every other template in the same industry.

What businesses in this area typically need:

- **A site that loads in under three seconds on mobile.** A lot of rural Illinois is on slower connections. A site that works fine on fiber in Chicago can be unusable on a phone in Durand.
- **Clear service pages, not a single homepage that lists everything.** Google ranks pages, not sites. A plumber with separate pages for "water heater repair" and "drain cleaning" will outrank one with a single page that mentions both in a paragraph.
- **Consistent NAP — name, address, phone — across the site and Google Business Profile.** If the phone number on the site does not match the one on Google Maps, local search ranking suffers.
- **Schema markup that tells Google what the business is and where it is.** Most template-built sites in this area do not have it. Adding it is not hard, but it makes a measurable difference in local pack rankings.

## How local SEO works in this region

Local SEO for Northwest Illinois is not about ranking for "web developer" — it is about helping local businesses rank for their own services in their own area. Here is what that actually involves:

1. **Google Business Profile optimization.** This is the single biggest factor in local pack rankings. The profile needs accurate categories, a service area list, photos, and regular posts. Most businesses in this area have a profile but never optimized it.
2. **On-page local signals.** The city name in the title tag, the H1, and the content. Not stuffed — present naturally. A service page that says "Water heater repair in Freeport, IL" once in the right places is enough.
3. **Schema markup.** `LocalBusiness` or `ProfessionalService` schema with the business name, address, phone, and service area. This is what tells Google the business is local and where it operates.
4. **Citations and NAP consistency.** The same name, address, and phone number on the site, on Google, on Yelp, on Facebook, and on industry directories. Inconsistencies confuse Google's local indexer.
5. **Reviews.** Not something I build, but something I advise on. A business with 15 Google reviews and a properly optimized site will outrank a business with 2 reviews and a better-looking site.

For my own local presence, I target "web developer Durand Illinois" and "web developer Davis Illinois" specifically because the competition is low enough that a well-structured page with local schema can rank. I do not pretend to rank in Rockford — that would be dishonest.
