## Why Monroe businesses compete across Green County

Monroe sits in southern Wisconsin, about an hour southwest of Madison and right on the Illinois border. US Route 69 and State Route 11 run through town, connecting Monroe to the broader Green County road network. When someone in Monroe searches for a plumber, a salon, or an HVAC company, they are not searching "Monroe." Half the time they are searching "near me," and Google decides what that means based on proximity, relevance, and the quality of your website and Google Business Profile.

That means a Monroe business is competing with companies from across Green County — Brodhead, New Glarus, Monticello, and the rural areas in between — and sometimes with businesses across the state line in northern Illinois. A website that was "good enough" in 2018 is not good enough now. You need a site that loads fast on mobile, has clean local SEO signals, and gives Google no reason to rank a competitor above you.

The good news: most of those competitors have slow, outdated websites too. A clean, modern, accessible build will outperform them. You do not need to outspend them. You need to out-execute them.

## The problem with companies that do not post prices

Most web design companies serving Monroe will not put prices on their website. There are two reasons.

The first is legitimate: every project is different, and a cheese shop on the square needs something different than a contractor serving the rural county. A fixed price list can feel misleading.

The second is not legitimate: they want you on a sales call so they can upsell you. If you do not know the going rate, you have no leverage. The longer they keep you in the dark, the more they can charge.

A web designer who posts prices is telling you they respect your time. A web designer who hides prices behind a "get a quote" form is telling you their sales process matters more than your budget. That is the biggest red flag in this industry.

## Why accessibility matters for Monroe businesses

ADA website lawsuits are not theoretical. Plaintiffs' firms have been sending demand letters to small businesses across Wisconsin for years, and Green County is not exempt. The cost of defending one — even if you win — is $5,000 to $25,000 in legal fees. The cost of building an accessible site from the start is $0 extra if your designer knows what they are doing.

Ask any designer: "Will my site meet WCAG 2.1 AA?" If they cannot answer that question specifically, do not hire them. "Accessible" is not an answer. "WCAG 2.1 AA, tested with axe and Lighthouse, with manual keyboard navigation checks" is an answer.

## Local SEO basics for Monroe

A website is not useful if it does not show up when someone searches "your service Monroe WI" or "your service Green County." Your site needs:

- **Google Business Profile.** Claim it, verify it, fill out every field, add photos, and get reviews. This is the single biggest local SEO lever for a Monroe business.
- **Local citations.** Your name, address, and phone (NAP) must be identical across your site, Google Business Profile, Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps, and industry directories. I cover this in my post on [local citations and NAP consistency](/local-citations-nap-consistency).
- **Service-area pages.** If you serve Monroe and the surrounding Green County communities, have a page for each. Not duplicate pages with the city name swapped — real, useful content about serving that area. I wrote a guide on [local landing pages without doorway spam](/local-landing-pages-without-doorway-spam).
- **Schema markup.** LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQPage schema help Google understand what you do and where.
- **Title tags and meta descriptions** with your service and city.

This should be included in the build, not sold as a separate $1,500 "SEO package."

### Wisconsin-specific local SEO notes

If your business serves customers on both sides of the state line — Monroe is close enough to northern Illinois that many businesses do — make sure your Google Business Profile and website content reflect your actual service area. Do not claim to serve areas you do not, but do not hide the fact that you serve both Wisconsin and Illinois customers. Clarity helps both Google and your customers.

## Questions to ask before hiring anyone for a Monroe website project

Print this list. Ask every one of these on the first call.

1. **What is the total cost, including everything?** Not "starting at." The actual number.
2. **What is included in the monthly fee, and what costs extra?**
3. **Will the site meet WCAG 2.1 AA?** Get it in writing.
4. **What will the mobile Lighthouse score be?** Anything under 80 is a problem.
5. **Do you set up Google Business Profile and schema markup, or is that extra?**
6. **Who owns the domain and hosting account?** It should be you, not them.
7. **What happens if I want to leave?** Can you take your site with you?
8. **Can I see three live sites you built, and run Lighthouse on them myself?**
9. **What is your timeline, and what delays it?**
10. **Do you have an accessibility statement on your own site?**

If a designer cannot answer these without hedging, keep looking.
