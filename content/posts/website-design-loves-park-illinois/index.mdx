## The problem with companies that do not post prices

Most web design companies serving Loves Park will not put prices on their website. There are two reasons.

The first is legitimate: every project is different, and a plumber needs something different than a law firm. A fixed price list can feel misleading.

The second is not legitimate: they want you on a sales call so they can upsell you. If you do not know the going rate, you have no leverage. The longer they keep you in the dark, the more they can charge.

A web designer who posts prices is telling you they respect your time. A web designer who hides prices behind a "get a quote" form is telling you their sales process matters more than your budget. That is the biggest red flag in this industry.

## Why accessibility matters for Loves Park businesses

ADA website lawsuits are not theoretical. Plaintiffs' firms have been sending demand letters to small businesses across Illinois for years, and the Rockford metro is not exempt. The cost of defending one — even if you win — is $5,000 to $25,000 in legal fees. The cost of building an accessible site from the start is $0 extra if your designer knows what they are doing.

Ask any designer: "Will my site meet WCAG 2.1 AA?" If they cannot answer that question specifically, do not hire them. "Accessible" is not an answer. "WCAG 2.1 AA, tested with axe and Lighthouse, with manual keyboard navigation checks" is an answer.

## How to evaluate a Loves Park web designer

A web designer's own website is their portfolio. If it is slow, inaccessible, or broken on mobile, that is what you are going to get.

Run this 60-second audit on any Loves Park web designer's site before you contact them:

1. Open their site on your phone. Does it load in under 3 seconds?
2. Run [Lighthouse](https://pagespeed.web.dev/) on their homepage. Are performance, accessibility, and SEO all 90+?
3. Try to navigate their site with only your keyboard (Tab key). Can you reach every link and button?
4. Check if they have an accessibility statement. If they do not, they are not taking it seriously.
5. Look at their blog. Is the most recent post within the last 3 months? A dead blog means a dead business.

If a designer's own site fails these checks, they cannot build a site that passes them for you.

## Questions to ask before hiring anyone for a Loves Park website project

Print this list. Ask every one of these on the first call.

1. **What is the total cost, including everything?** Not "starting at." The actual number.
2. **What is included in the monthly fee, and what costs extra?**
3. **Will the site meet WCAG 2.1 AA?** Get it in writing.
4. **What will the mobile Lighthouse score be?** Anything under 80 is a problem.
5. **Do you set up Google Business Profile and schema markup, or is that extra?**
6. **Who owns the domain and hosting account?** It should be you, not them.
7. **What happens if I want to leave?** Can you take your site with you?
8. **Can I see three live sites you built, and run Lighthouse on them myself?**
9. **What is your timeline, and what delays it?**
10. **Do you have an accessibility statement on your own site?**

If a designer cannot answer these without hedging, keep looking.

## Local SEO basics for Loves Park

A website is not useful if it does not show up when someone searches "your service Loves Park IL" or "your service Rockford IL." Your site needs:

- **Google Business Profile.** Claim it, verify it, fill out every field, add photos, and get reviews. This is the single biggest local SEO lever for a Loves Park business.
- **Local citations.** Your name, address, and phone (NAP) must be identical across your site, Google Business Profile, Yelp, Bing Places, Apple Maps, and industry directories. I cover this in my post on [local citations and NAP consistency](/local-citations-nap-consistency).
- **Service-area pages.** If you serve both Loves Park and Rockford, have a page for each. Not duplicate pages with the city name swapped — real, useful content about serving that area. I wrote a guide on [local landing pages without doorway spam](/local-landing-pages-without-doorway-spam).
- **Schema markup.** LocalBusiness, Service, and FAQPage schema help Google understand what you do and where.
- **Title tags and meta descriptions** with your service and city.

This should be included in the build, not sold as a separate $1,500 "SEO package."
