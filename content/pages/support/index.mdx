## How do I get help with a download or payment issue?

If you need help with a download, payment, or access issue, email **bradmatera@gmail.com** with your order ID. Most issues are resolved within 24 hours. Include your order ID, the email used at checkout, and a description of the problem so support can locate your purchase quickly.

## What is the refund policy for digital downloads?

Digital downloads are generally non-refundable once accessed. If a file is broken or missing, contact support within 7 days of purchase and we will fix the issue or issue a full refund. Affiliate products are sold by third-party merchants and follow their respective refund policies.

## Common Issues

- **Missing download link:** Return to your success link from Stripe or refresh the `/success` page with the same `session_id`.
- **Need to view purchases:** Go to `/purchases` (customer portal) and sign in.
- **Payment not verified:** Stripe may still be processing. Wait a minute and refresh.
- **Need a new link:** Support can rotate files and issue a new link if needed.
