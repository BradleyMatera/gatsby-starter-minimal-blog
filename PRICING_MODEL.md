## Bradley's Pricing Model

All prices are designed to undercut N5 Studio (the cheapest competitor) by approximately 10–15%
while remaining fair to Bradley's skill level and time investment.

### Tier 1: Starter — $447 build + $37/month support

**Rationale:**
- Undercuts N5 Studio's $497 by $50 (10% discount)
- Undercuts N5 Studio's $47/mo by $10 (21% discount)
- Fair for a junior developer building a 5-page static site
- Estimated time: 10–14 hours at ~$45/hr effective rate for the build portion
- Monthly support covers hosting monitoring, small content updates, and security patches
- Estimated monthly time: 1–2 hours

**Includes:**
- Up to 5 pages (home, about, services, contact, +1)
- Mobile-responsive, SEO-ready
- Contact form with spam protection
- Hosting setup (Netlify or equivalent)
- Google Search Console + sitemap submission
- Schema markup (LocalBusiness, Service)
- 2 rounds of revisions during build
- 14-day build time
- 30-day post-launch warranty
- 24-hour response time guarantee

**Tax breakdown of $447 build fee:**
- Labor (10 hrs × $35/hr): $350
- Software/tools amortization: $20
- Hosting setup labor (1 hr × $35/hr): $35
- Project management/communication (1 hr × $35/hr): $35
- Contingency/buffer: $7
- **Total:** $447

**Tax breakdown of $37/month support:**
- Labor (1.5 hrs × $20/hr): $30
- Hosting infrastructure pass-through: $5
- Software/tools amortization: $2
- **Total:** $37

## Payment Terms

- **Deposit:** 50% upfront to reserve slot and cover initial work
- **Balance:** 50% due at launch
- **Monthly support:** Billed monthly, starts when site goes live
- **Monthly support is month-to-month** — no long-term contract
- **Cancellation:** Client owns all code, content, and domain. Help with migration at no extra charge.

## Response Time Guarantee

- **Starter and Growth tiers:** 24-hour response time guarantee
- **Premium tier:** Same-day response during business hours (9am–5pm CT)
- **If response time is missed:** Client receives $25 credit toward next month's support

## What's NOT Included (À la carte)

- **Domain registration:** ~$15/year, paid directly to registrar
- **Logo and branding:** Custom quoted, referred to specialist
- **Custom photography:** Custom quoted
- **Paid ad management:** Custom quoted based on ad spend
- **E-commerce functionality:** Custom quoted based on complexity
- **3-day rush delivery:** +$150 to any tier
- **Major redesigns after launch:** Quoted per project

## Tax Notes

- All prices are in USD
- Sales tax: Illinois does not require sales tax on custom software development services
- Income tax: All revenue is self-employment income subject to SE tax (15.3%) + federal/state income tax
- Track all business expenses (software, hosting, equipment, home office) for deductions
- Quarterly estimated tax payments required if expected tax liability > $1,000
- Use Schedule C (Profit or Loss from Business) for sole proprietorship
- Keep receipts and documentation for all business expenses
